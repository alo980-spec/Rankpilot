FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I use RankPilot for free?

Yes. The prototype includes a free audit flow. Paid plans are designed for deeper audits and monitoring.

Does a good score guarantee Google rankings?

No. SEO tools can identify technical and content opportunities, but no tool can guarantee a specific Google ranking.

Can it analyze real websites?

This first version is a zero-cost prototype. The next phase can connect a secure crawler and services such as PageSpeed Insights and Search Console.
